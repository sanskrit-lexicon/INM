## When to use INM

Use INM for **proper name lookup** in the Mahābhārata context. For general Sanskrit vocabulary, prefer MW.

---

## Relationship to MW

INM is a name index rather than a full dictionary. It indexes proper names occurring in the Mahābhārata, useful for tracing mythological figures and their appearances across the epic. Complements MW's biographical entries and the INM-specific `<bio>` block. [DRAFT — maintainers please verify the author (Sørensen 1904?) and scope.]
